Pushkin studio website

About the project and results

The website launched back in 2016, but its carefully chosen neutral UI, solid UX, well-prepared bitmap images, and fresh mechanics for that time allowed it to work for almost six years without major redesign. The content kept changing, and the site continued to live. For a web studio in a dynamic and competitive market, that is a long lifespan for a design. During that time, the website converted a large number of leads and received enthusiastic feedback from clients; the design was memorable and did its job. In 2022, the studio concept was revised and the website was replaced with a new one.
